(06) - Questions

Before you DM.

What counts as a “digital product”?

Anything that solves one specific problem for a specific person without you being live in the room: templates, toolkits, guides, swipe files, mini-courses, prompts, audits or community memberships.

How big does my audience need to be?

Smaller than you think. A few hundred people who trust you and keep asking the same question beats ten thousand passive followers. We check the signal before we commit to anything.

How long until I can sell something?

The Sprint is planned to get you to an open cart in about [three weeks]. The build is fast on purpose - momentum matters more than polish on version one.

Do I need a big email list or a website?

No. Instagram DMs and a simple checkout link are enough for your first launch. We'll add the funnel once there's real demand to point it at.

Which level do I actually need?

That depends on how much of the build you want off your plate. If you just need clarity, Level 01. If you need it shipped, Level 02. If you want it shipped and sold, Level 03.

You're a new agency - why should I trust you?

Fair question, and we won't dodge it. We don't have a wall of case studies yet, which is exactly why founding creators get a better price and the founders' full attention. You'll talk to the person doing the work, see every step as it happens, and only appear as a case study if you're happy with the result.

What does it cost?

Each level shows a starting price on its card. The final number depends on how much of the build we take on, and we'll confirm it in writing before anything starts - no surprises. Founding creators get [X]% off any level.

What if my product doesn't sell?

We can't promise sales - anyone who does is guessing. What we do is validate demand before you build, using your saves, comments and DMs, so you launch something people have already asked for. [If you offer a guarantee or a free relaunch review, state it here.]
