(04) · How it works✳

Six steps.
One shared
direction.

From the first conversation to the first launch, and what happens after it.

  1. 01Meet

    Start with your world.

    We learn your expertise, your audience and what you want to build. Then we both decide whether this is a fit.

  2. 02Research

    Find the recurring need.

    We go through everything you have posted and map it against what your audience actually engages with. Comments, saves and DMs show the pattern. We look at Instagram and YouTube, including what people ask after watching.

  3. 03Validate

    Test the idea first.

    The repeated question, the saved post, the DM that lands twice a week. That repetition is the offer. A focused promise, a useful sample and an interest page tell us whether it deserves a full build. Interest guides the decision. It does not guarantee sales.

  4. 04Build

    Make the useful thing.

    We research, write, structure, design and build the complete product: name, promise, price, content, cover, layout and finished files. Your content, brand and niche guide it. You review and approve.

  5. 05Launch

    Bring it to your people.

    A content run that points at one call to action, a DM script that qualifies the people who reply, a product page and an email sequence. It should sound like you and say plainly what the product helps with.

  6. 06Improve

    Learn, then change one thing.

    Customer questions and real responses show what to fix. We review the offer and the experience together, then agree the next useful change.

(11) · Before you DM✳

Good
questions.
Straight
answers.

Clarity comes before commitment.

What counts as a digital product?

Anything that solves one specific problem for one specific person without you being live in the room. Guides and ebooks, creator-led courses, workbooks, toolkits, templates for your audience to use, swipe files or a community membership. We decide the format after the research, not before it.

Do you build it, or do I?

We build it. We research your audience and niche, write the original content, structure it, design it and prepare the finished files. You review accuracy, approve the direction and share it with your audience. On courses with video teaching you stay the creator on camera, and we develop the curriculum, the lesson scripts and the agreed production around you.

Is it really custom, or is it a template with my name on it?

Custom. We create the full product from the ground up, including what goes inside it and how it looks. There is no template pack. We research your audience and niche, use your content and expertise to guide the original writing, and design everything around your brand.

How big does my audience need to be?

Smaller than you think. Our focus right now is creators with roughly 2,000–100,000 followers or subscribers, but we also look at trust, activity, expertise and recurring demand. A few hundred people who keep asking the same question beats ten thousand passive followers. We check the signal before we commit to anything.

What do I actually have to do?

Hand over your existing content and expertise, review the work for accuracy, approve the direction, and share the finished product with your audience. For courses with video, you record the teaching. Everything else sits with us.

Can I keep posting my usual content?

Yes. That is the point. You keep creating while we research, write, structure, design and build the product behind the scenes. We agree upfront how much of your time the review and the launch will need.

How long until I can sell something?

The Sprint is planned to get you to an open cart in about [WEEKS]. The build is fast on purpose. On version one, momentum matters more than polish.

Do I need an email list or a website?

No. Instagram DMs and a simple checkout link are enough for a first launch. We add the funnel once there is real demand to point it at.

Which level do I actually need?

It depends on how much of the build you want off your plate. If you need clarity, Level 01. If you need it shipped, Level 02. If you want it shipped and sold, Level 03. The three-tap check on this page will point you at one.

What does it cost?

Each level shows a starting price on its card. The final number depends on how much of the build we take on, and we confirm it in writing before anything starts. No surprises. Founding creators get [X]% off any level while the first spots are open.

You’re a new agency. Why should I trust you?

Fair question, and we are not going to dodge it. We do not have a wall of case studies yet, which is exactly why founding creators get a better deal and the founders’ full attention. You talk to the person doing the work, you see every step as it happens, and you only appear as a case study if you are happy with the result.

What if the product doesn’t sell?

We cannot promise sales, and anyone who does is guessing. Sales depend on demand, audience response, the offer and the launch. What we do is validate demand before we build, using your saves, comments and DMs, so you launch something people have already asked for.
