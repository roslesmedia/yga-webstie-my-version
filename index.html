    Privacy policy

    This policy explains what personal data YGA — Your Growth Agency (“we”) handles when you visit this website or contact us, why, and what rights you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

    Who is responsible

    The controller is YGA — Your Growth Agency, based in Slovakia. Contact for anything privacy-related: agency.yga@gmail.com. Full business details are in the legal notice.

    What this website does not do

    • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking of any kind.
    • No forms, accounts or uploads: the website itself does not ask you for any personal data.
    • No chatbot and no AI features on the website.

    If we ever add analytics, a booking form or a newsletter, we will update this policy first, and anything that is not strictly necessary will only run after you agree to it.

    What we do process, and why

    • Technical data when you load the site (IP address, browser type, date and time, page requested). Our hosting provider (and jsDelivr, which serves one styling script) process this automatically to deliver the site and keep it secure. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
    • Messages you send us by Instagram DM or email (your name or handle, contact details and whatever you choose to tell us about your audience and project). We use them only to answer you and, if you want, to prepare and carry out a project. Legal basis: steps you ask for before a contract and the contract itself (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
    • Invoices and contracts, if we work together, to meet Slovak accounting and tax law. Legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
    • One setting in your browser: if you press the pause-animations button, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage on your own device. It is not a cookie, it is not sent to us, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

    We only ask for what we need. Please don’t send us sensitive information (for example about health) that a project doesn’t require.

    Who else receives data

    • Vercel Inc. (USA): hosts this website and keeps short-lived server logs.
    • jsDelivr, a public open-source content delivery network run on several network providers, some outside the EU: delivers the one styling script the page uses, so it sees your IP address and browser details. We have no contract with it; we rely on our legitimate interest in loading the site.
    • Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd (Instagram) handles DMs you send us there, under Instagram’s own privacy policy.
    • Google Ireland Ltd (Gmail) handles emails you send us.

    Some of these providers are based outside the EU. Vercel is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; for other transfers to the USA, the providers rely on that framework or on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

    AI tools

    The website doesn’t use AI. During a project we may use AI-assisted tools to help sort and summarise audience comments and questions you share with us. We tell you which tools before we use them, we don’t put your data into tools that train on it, and you can ask us not to use them at all. When we analyse your audience’s comments for you, we do so on your instructions under a data processing agreement, and we remove names and handles wherever we can.

    How long we keep it

    • Server logs: as long as the hosting provider keeps them for security, usually a few days to a few weeks.
    • Messages and project notes: as long as we’re talking or working together, then at most 24 months after our last contact, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
    • Invoices and contracts: as long as Slovak accounting and tax law requires.

    Your rights

    You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its use, and receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on legitimate interest, you can object at any time. You don’t have to give us any data; without contact details we simply can’t reply or work with you. We don’t make automated decisions about you or profile you. You can also complain to the Slovak supervisory authority, the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR, dataprotection.gov.sk).

    To delete your data: email agency.yga@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request” and tell us which handle or email you used. We confirm within one month and delete everything we are not legally required to keep.

    Children

    Our services are only for people aged 18 or over and for businesses. We don’t knowingly process data of children; if you think a child has contacted us, tell us and we will delete it.

    Changes

    If what we do with data changes, we update this page and the date below before the change takes effect.
