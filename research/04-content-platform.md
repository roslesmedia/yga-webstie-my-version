### D1 implementation — name is one find/replace

Even though the name is locked, keep it swappable. There are exactly **9** places the full name appears. Nowhere else may hardcode it.

1. `<title>` 2. `<meta name="description">` 3. `og:site_name` 4. `og:title` 5. `twitter:title` 6. JSON-LD `Organization.name` 7. masthead/logo `aria-label` 8. footer wordmark block 9. footer legal line.

In the body copy the brand is written **`YGA`** only. Never "Your Growth Agency" in a sentence. The expansion lives in the masthead and footer, which is also how a reader learns it. One find/replace of the string `Your Growth Agency` changes the whole site.

The "Your" in the name is now doing double duty with the second-person voice. **Guard rail:** never put the name next to a sentence that starts with "Your". The hero H1 starts "You have", the footer wordmark is stacked `Your / Growth / Agency`, and nothing between them says "Your".

### D2 copy guard rails (apply to every string below)

- **Dashes.** No em-dashes. Use a period, a comma or a colon. En-dash only inside a number range (`2,000–100,000`). Source B's copy is full of em-dashes (`start to finish—and what comes after`); every one is removed below.
- **No triplet taglines.** Source B's `Ideas. Products. Possibilities.` / `Good people. Bold ideas. Shared growth.` / `Make it useful. Make it yours. Make it happen.` / `Curiosity → Clarity → Creation` are all **cut**. They are the single loudest AI tell in source B.
- **Quotes and apostrophes stay typographic:** `’ “ ”`. That is correct typesetting, not an AI tell. Straight quotes `' "` only inside code.
- **Numbers.** The only hard numbers permitted on the page are `2,000–100,000` followers (client's own stated focus in B) and the `01`–`06` step indices. Everything else is a bracket placeholder.
- **No claims.** No "award", no "trusted by", no "X creators served", no star ratings, no logo wall. The page's credibility move is the opposite: it admits there are no case studies yet.
- Banned additions on top of the client list: `dive in`, `supercharge`, `cutting-edge`, `robust`, `at scale`, `transform your`, `empower`, `craft` (verb), `bespoke` in body copy (use "custom" / "built for you"; "bespoke" is fine in the internal spec, not on the page).

### D6 — OPEN DECISION the client must still make: the commercial model

The two sources promise **mutually exclusive** money mechanics and both cannot sit on one page.

- **A** sells three levels, each `From [€ PRICE]`, with `[X]%` off for founding creators.
- **B** says `0 upfront agency fees` and `We only earn when you do`, an agreed revenue share.

Printing both is an immediate trust failure: a reader sees "no upfront fees" above "From [€ PRICE]" and leaves.

**Recommendation: ship Variant P (priced levels) as the spine, and offer the revenue share as the Level 03 alternative.** Reasons: the brief keeps A's conversion architecture; three concrete levels qualify leads and set expectations; a revenue-share-only model with no case studies is a hard sell and exposes YGA to doing full production for nothing. The share then becomes an upside option on the deepest engagement, which is where it actually makes sense.

Section 9 below gives the **complete copy for both variants**. Pick one, delete the other. Every string that differs is tagged `[P]` or `[R]`.

Notes that matter:

- **Title** is 62 characters. Treat 50–60 as a lint proxy, not a limit; Google truncates to the rendered device width and may build its own title link from the H1 regardless. The H1 (`You have the audience. We build what’s next.`) and the title agree on topic, which is what reduces rewriting.
- **`og:locale`** is `en_GB` to match the copy (`optimisation`, `organisation`, `£/€`). Switch to `en_US` only if the copy is Americanised, and change `<html lang>` to match.
- **`og:image` must be a real raster file.** Social scrapers do not execute JavaScript or render SVG. JPEG, not WebP: several scrapers including some WhatsApp and LinkedIn paths still fail on WebP. 1200×630, under 300 KB. Versioned filename (`og-v1.jpg`) so it can be cached immutably and busted by renaming.
- **`color-scheme: light`** stops Chrome and Safari auto-darkening form controls and scrollbars on a light-only design. Without it, `prefers-color-scheme: dark` users get inverted native UI against the beige.
- **`format-detection`** stops iOS Safari turning `[SPOTS]`, `[X]%` and date-like strings into blue tappable links.
- **No `<meta name="generator">`.** D3.
- **No `keywords` meta.** Ignored, and reads as low-effort SEO.

### 4.3 Favicon strategy

| Asset | Format | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary icon | SVG, inline `data:` URI | `<link rel="icon">` | Zero requests, crisp at every size, supported in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari 16.4+. Spaces are `%20` and `#` is `%23`, which is what makes the URI legal. |
| iOS home screen | `apple-touch-icon.png`, 180×180 | root file | Safari ignores SVG here. Only non-text binary besides the OG image. |
| Legacy `.ico` | **skip** | — | Browsers that need it are below the support floor. A missing `/favicon.ico` returns 404 silently from the browser's own probe and does not fail any audit. |

The icon is the YGA Y-glyph from source B, recoloured to Pine `#00311F` with a Gold `#E5C690` letterform. Gold on Pine measures **8.78:1**, so the mark stays legible at 16px. Gold on the beige would be 1.54:1 and would disappear.

### 4.7 On-page SEO rules

- **One `<h1>`**, the hero. Sections use `<h2>`, cards `<h3>`. No level skipped. The `.sr-only` copy inside a display heading is what carries the heading's text, so the hierarchy is intact for crawlers and for screen readers alike.
- Section eyebrows (`(01) · The gap`) are `<p class="eyebrow">`, never headings.
- `<section>` elements each need an accessible name: `aria-labelledby` pointing at their `<h2>`'s id. An unnamed `<section>` is not announced as a region.
- Anchor text is descriptive. No `Click here`, no bare `Read more`. `See how it works`, `Compare the levels`, `Take the three-tap check`.
- All outbound links: `rel="noopener"`. Instagram gets `rel="noopener me"` so the `sameAs` relationship is machine-readable both ways.
- Instagram DM links use `https://ig.me/m/[IG HANDLE]` (opens the DM thread directly on mobile, falls back to the web profile on desktop) with the keyword in the visible label, since the keyword cannot be prefilled.
- `[CONTACT EMAIL]` is a `mailto:` with a prefilled subject: `mailto:[CONTACT EMAIL]?subject=GROWTH`. That keeps A's keyword mechanic working off-platform.
- Decorative glyphs (`✳ ↗ ↔ ◎ ▷ ↻ ×`) are wrapped in `<span aria-hidden="true">`. Unhidden, a screen reader reads "asterisk operator, north east arrow" between every eyebrow and headline.

## 5. Vercel deployment — zero config, zero build, zero errors

"Deploys with no errors" is the explicit client requirement, so this section is written as a procedure, not advice.

### 5.1 Exact repo layout

```
/                              <- Vercel Root Directory = ./
├── index.html                 <- the entire site. All CSS and JS inline.
├── og-v1.jpg                  <- 1200x630 JPEG, <300 KB
├── apple-touch-icon.png       <- 180x180 PNG
├── site.webmanifest
├── robots.txt
├── sitemap.xml
├── vercel.json                <- headers only. See 5.4.
├── privacy.html               <- optional, only if the footer links to it
├── README.md
├── .gitignore
└── reference/                 <- source A archive. Harmless; served but unlinked.
```

Eight files plus one optional page. That is the whole deployment.

**What must NOT be in the repo root:**

| Must not exist | Why |
|---|---|
| `package.json` | This is the single biggest cause of a failed static deploy. Its presence makes Vercel's zero-config detection treat the repo as a Node project and run `npm install` then `npm run build`. With no `build` script it errors; with source B's `build` script it runs `node scripts/build.mjs`, which is not in this repo, and the deploy fails. |
| `package-lock.json`, `pnpm-lock.yaml`, `yarn.lock`, `bun.lockb` | Each one pins an install step and can force a package manager that then fails. |
| `api/` | Vercel auto-detects any file under `api/` as a Serverless Function. Source B has `api/apply.js` and `api/config.js`. Copied in, they are compiled at build time, and a missing dependency or an unsupported runtime fails the whole deploy. |
| `server.mjs`, `lib/`, `scripts/`, `tests/` | Dead weight from source B. `scripts/build.mjs` referenced by a stray `package.json` is a guaranteed failure. |
| `public/` | With Framework Preset "Other", if `public/` exists Vercel serves **that** as the output directory, and the root `index.html` is never served. The site would 404 while the build reports success. |
| `.vercel/` | Local project link. Already covered by `.gitignore`. |
| `next.config.js`, `vite.config.js`, `astro.config.mjs`, any framework config | Triggers framework detection and a build. |

`.gitignore` stays as it is (`node_modules/`, `.DS_Store`, `.vercel`) and must **not** grow a pattern that matches `og-v1.jpg`, `apple-touch-icon.png` or `*.html`. A gitignored asset becomes a 404 in production with no error at build time. Verify with `git check-ignore -v og-v1.jpg apple-touch-icon.png index.html` (no output means all three are tracked).

### 5.2 Source B's `vercel.json` must not be copied. It would break the site twice.

`/home/user/refs/yga-agency-site-/yga-website-2/vercel.json` contains three things that are fatal here:

1. `"buildCommand": "npm run build"` and `"outputDirectory": "dist"` — no build script, no `dist`. **Deploy fails.**
2. `"functions": { "api/*.js": { "maxDuration": 30 } }` — a `functions` pattern matching no file is a configuration error. **Deploy fails.**
3. `"Content-Security-Policy": "... script-src 'self'; style-src 'self' ..."` — this blocks every inline `<script>` and `<style>`. On a single-file site that is **the entire page**. The deploy would succeed and the site would render as unstyled text with a console full of CSP violations. This is the worst failure mode of the three because nothing reports it.

Write a new `vercel.json` from scratch. Do not edit theirs.

### 5.4 `vercel.json` — needed? Yes, for headers only

A bare `index.html` at root deploys and serves with **no `vercel.json` at all**. It is needed for one reason: cache and security headers. Vercel's defaults are reasonable for HTML but leave the static assets under-cached and send no security headers.

```json
{
  "$schema": "https://openapi.vercel.sh/vercel.json",
  "framework": null,
  "buildCommand": "",
  "outputDirectory": ".",
  "cleanUrls": true,
  "trailingSlash": false,
  "headers": [
    {
      "source": "/(.*)",
      "headers": [
        { "key": "X-Content-Type-Options", "value": "nosniff" },
        { "key": "Referrer-Policy", "value": "strict-origin-when-cross-origin" },
        { "key": "Permissions-Policy", "value": "camera=(), microphone=(), geolocation=(), payment=(), usb=()" },
        { "key": "Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy", "value": "same-origin" },
        { "key": "X-Frame-Options", "value": "SAMEORIGIN" }
      ]
    },
    {
      "source": "/",
      "headers": [
        { "key": "Cache-Control", "value": "public, max-age=0, must-revalidate" },
        { "key": "Content-Security-Policy", "value": "default-src 'self'; script-src 'self' 'unsafe-inline'; style-src 'self' 'unsafe-inline'; img-src 'self' data:; font-src 'self' data:; connect-src 'self'; frame-src 'none'; object-src 'none'; base-uri 'self'; form-action 'none'; frame-ancestors 'self'; upgrade-insecure-requests" }
      ]
    },
    {
      "source": "/index.html",
      "headers": [
        { "key": "Cache-Control", "value": "public, max-age=0, must-revalidate" }
      ]
    },
    {
      "source": "/og-v1.jpg",
      "headers": [
        { "key": "Cache-Control", "value": "public, max-age=31536000, immutable" }
      ]
    },
    {
      "source": "/apple-touch-icon.png",
      "headers": [
        { "key": "Cache-Control", "value": "public, max-age=31536000, immutable" }
      ]
    },
    {
      "source": "/site.webmanifest",
      "headers": [
        { "key": "Cache-Control", "value": "public, max-age=604800, stale-while-revalidate=86400" }
      ]
    },
    {
      "source": "/(robots.txt|sitemap.xml)",
      "headers": [
        { "key": "Cache-Control", "value": "public, max-age=3600, stale-while-revalidate=86400" }
      ]
    }
  ]

```

**Why each cache value:**

- **HTML: `max-age=0, must-revalidate`.** The HTML *is* the application. Any positive `max-age` means a copy edit, a filled-in price or a new founding-spot count is invisible to returning visitors until the cache expires. Vercel's edge still caches it server-side and serves it fast; `must-revalidate` only governs the browser.
- **`og-v1.jpg` and `apple-touch-icon.png`: `immutable` for a year.** Safe **only** because the filename is versioned. To change the social card, upload `og-v2.jpg` and update the two meta tags. Never overwrite `og-v1.jpg` in place: with `immutable` nobody will ever see the new one.
- **Manifest and robots/sitemap: short, with `stale-while-revalidate`.** They change occasionally and nothing breaks if a revalidation is a few hours late.
- **No `Strict-Transport-Security`.** Vercel already sends HSTS on `*.vercel.app` and on custom domains it manages. Adding it by hand risks pinning HTTPS on a subdomain that is not ready, and that is not reversible for a user who has already cached it. Confirm what is actually sent with `curl -I` before adding anything.

**Why the CSP is scoped to `/` and `/index.html`, not `/(.*)`:** the policy's `script-src 'self' 'unsafe-inline'` is what makes the inline `<script>` run at all. `'unsafe-inline'` is weaker than a nonce, but a nonce requires a server to generate it per response, which a static file cannot do. The honest tradeoff on a page with no user input, no third-party script and no form POST is: accept `'unsafe-inline'`, and tighten everything else hard. `form-action 'none'` and `object-src 'none'` do real work here. Test with `Content-Security-Policy-Report-Only` first if there is any doubt.

**If a CDN script is added** (GSAP, Three.js — see §7), the CSP must gain that exact origin in `script-src`, and `connect-src` too if the library fetches anything. Forgetting this is the second most common way a Vercel static deploy looks broken while reporting success. The preferred answer is to add no CDN script at all.

## 6. Accessibility and Core Web Vitals checklist

Written for this page specifically: heavy entrance motion, oversized split display type, a WebGL canvas, a sticky nav, a scroll-progress bar and a marquee.

### 6.1 LCP

The LCP element on this page will be the **hero H1 text**, because there is no hero image. That is the easy case, and there are exactly three ways to ruin it.

1. **Never animate the hero H1 from `opacity: 0`.** An element at zero opacity is not a paint, so the browser either records LCP late or picks a different, smaller element. The hero headline renders at full opacity on first paint. Its entrance is **`transform` only** (`translateY`, `rotateX`, `scale`), which still counts as painted. Every other section may animate opacity freely, because none of them are LCP candidates.
2. **The display font must not block the H1.** Two acceptable routes:
   - *Recommended:* inline the YGA Signal face as a base64 `woff2` inside the `<style>` block. It is 12 KB as `.woff` in source B, roughly 8–9 KB recompressed to `woff2`, about 12 KB as base64. With no network request, `font-display` is irrelevant and there is no swap, no FOUT and no font-driven LCP delay. The face is CC0 per `ASSET-LICENSE.md`, so there is no licensing obstacle.
   - *Alternative:* ship `yga-signal-v1.woff2` as a root file with `<link rel="preload" as="font" type="font/woff2" crossorigin>` and `font-display: swap`, plus the metric-matched fallback in §6.2.
   Body copy uses the system stack and loads nothing: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`. That is what source B does and it is correct here.
3. **The WebGL canvas must not become the LCP candidate.** A `<canvas>` is eligible. Keep it from winning by initialising it **after first paint**: paint its box with a CSS gradient in the brand colours so there is no flash, then start WebGL from `requestIdleCallback` (fallback `setTimeout(init, 200)`) or on the `load` event. The gradient is the visual; the shader is the enhancement.

Also: TTFB is Vercel's edge, well under 800 ms for a static file. Critical CSS is the whole CSS and it is inline, so there is no render-blocking request. Nothing is render-blocking except the HTML itself. There is no second page, so **no Speculation Rules** — prerendering has nothing to prerender.

Target: LCP ≤ 1.5 s on a throttled mobile lab run, which leaves room against the 2.5 s p75 threshold.

### 6.2 CLS

| Risk on this page | Fix |
|---|---|
| The WebGL canvas resizing after init | Reserve the box in CSS with `aspect-ratio` plus a fixed `height`. Set the canvas `width`/`height` **attributes** to the backing-store size and the CSS size separately. Never let JS set the element's layout size. |
| Display font swapping in | If the face is not inlined, add a metric-matched fallback so the swap does not reflow the oversized headlines: `@font-face { font-family: "YGA Fallback"; src: local("Arial Black"), local("Impact"); size-adjust: 104%; ascent-override: 88%; descent-override: 16%; line-gap-override: 0%; }` then `font-family: "YGA Signal", "YGA Fallback", sans-serif`. Tune `size-adjust` until a screenshot before and after the swap shows no line-box movement. Inlining the font removes this risk entirely, which is why it is recommended. |
| Entrance animations | `transform` and `opacity` only. Never animate `height`, `width`, `top`, `margin`, `padding` or `font-size`. Those are the only properties that can produce layout shift. |
| GSAP ScrollTrigger pinning | `pinSpacing` injects a spacer element after layout, which shifts everything below. If anything is pinned, use `position: sticky` inside a tall wrapper instead, so the space is reserved by CSS at parse time. |
| Sticky nav changing height on scroll | Fix `--nav-h` and never change it. If the nav condenses, animate `transform: scale()` on its inner content, not its height. |
| The marquee | Width must be stable. Duplicate the content to at least 2× the viewport and translate it; never append nodes at runtime. |
| `100vh` on mobile | Use `100svh` for the hero `min-height`. `dvh` tracks the URL bar and causes a shift every time it hides. |
| `content-visibility: auto` on below-fold sections | Real win on initial render cost, real CLS risk if `contain-intrinsic-size` is wrong. Only apply it with a measured `contain-intrinsic-size` per section, and re-measure after copy changes. It also breaks in-page find and anchor scrolling if the estimate is far off. If in doubt, leave it off: the page is not heavy enough to need it. |
| `<details>` FAQ opening | Not counted against CLS when it happens within 500 ms of a user interaction. Safe. |
| Filled placeholders changing line counts | `[X]%` → `25%` can rewrap a headline. Not a CLS event, but re-check the display line breaks after the client fills the brackets. |
| The `Your match` ribbon appearing after the quiz | Reserve its space in the card with a `min-height`, or render it in a slot that already exists at `visibility: hidden`. |

Target: CLS 0.00. On a page with no ads, no embeds, no lazy iframes and no web font in the critical path, zero is achievable and anything above 0.02 means a bug.

### 6.3 INP

| Risk | Fix |
|---|---|
| Scroll handlers | No `scroll` listener does layout work. Use `IntersectionObserver` for entrances and, where supported, CSS `animation-timeline: view()` so the entrance costs the main thread nothing at all. Feed the progress bar from a single rAF-throttled read. |
| Listener options | `{ passive: true }` on every `scroll`, `wheel`, `touchstart` and `touchmove` listener. Without it Chrome must wait to see whether the handler calls `preventDefault()`. |
| WebGL render loop | Cap DPR at `min(devicePixelRatio, 1.5)`. Pause via `IntersectionObserver` when the canvas leaves the viewport. Pause on `document.visibilityState === "hidden"`. Render one frame and stop under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` or `data-motion="off"`. An uncapped loop on a 3× phone is the single largest INP risk on this page. |
| Long tasks at load | Initialise in stages: hero first, then everything else from `requestIdleCallback`. Yield inside any loop over more than ~50 nodes with `await (scheduler.yield?.() ?? new Promise(r => setTimeout(r, 0)))`. |
| FAQ and the five "what we do" steps | Native `<details>` / `<summary>`. Browser-native disclosure is near-zero INP and arrives with keyboard support, `aria-expanded` and SR semantics already correct. Do not rebuild it in JS. |
| The quiz | Toggle classes and `hidden` on pre-rendered steps. Do not rebuild the DOM per answer. |
| Cursor follower / magnetic buttons | Only behind `matchMedia("(pointer: fine)")` and `(prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, and only rAF-throttled. On touch it is pure cost. |
| Mobile menu | `<dialog>` with `showModal()`, or a CSS-only checkbox-free toggle. Avoid locking scroll with `position: fixed` on `<body>`: it jumps the scroll position on close. Use `overflow: hidden` on the html element and restore `scrollTop`. |

Target: INP ≤ 100 ms. The interactions on this page are a nav toggle, eight disclosures, three quiz taps and some anchor links. There is no reason to be anywhere near 200 ms.

### 6.4 Reduced motion and the motion toggle

```css
@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) {
  *, *::before, *::after {
    animation-duration: 0.01ms !important;
    animation-iteration-count: 1 !important;
    transition-duration: 0.01ms !important;
    scroll-behavior: auto !important;
  }
}
```

That block alone is not enough here. Four more things:

1. **Content must never be gated behind an animation.** Reveal classes may only hide content when JavaScript is running *and* motion is allowed. Set the gate from an inline script in `<head>`:
   ```html
   <script>
     var r = document.documentElement;
     r.classList.add('js');
     try { if (localStorage.getItem('yga-motion') === 'off') r.dataset.motion = 'off'; } catch (e) {}
   </script>
   ```
   ```css
   .reveal { opacity: 1; transform: none; }
   @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference) {
     html.js:not([data-motion="off"]) .reveal { opacity: 0; transform: translateY(2rem); }
   }
   ```
   With JS off, with motion reduced, or with the toggle off, every word is visible. This also means a failed script never produces a blank page, which is the standard way heavy-motion sites break.
2. **Focus reveals immediately.** `.reveal:focus-within { opacity: 1 !important; transform: none !important; transition: none; }` Otherwise tabbing into an un-revealed section lands focus on invisible content, which fails WCAG 2.4.11.
3. **The WebGL scene stays visible, frozen.** Under reduced motion, render exactly one frame and stop the loop. Do not hide the canvas: the visual is part of the design and removing it changes the layout.
4. **A visible motion toggle**, from source B. Real `<button>` with `aria-pressed`, in the masthead and reachable as roughly the third tab stop. It writes `localStorage` inside `try/catch` (private mode throws) and sets `data-motion` on `<html>`. Label text changes between `Motion on` and `Motion off`, so state is conveyed by text and not only by an icon. Any effect that runs longer than five seconds unprompted (the marquee, the WebGL loop, the carousel mock) must respond to it. That is WCAG 2.2.2.

### 6.5 Keyboard navigation and focus

- **Skip link** first in the DOM, visible on focus, targets `<main id="main" tabindex="-1">`.
- **Native elements throughout.** `<a href>` for anything that navigates, `<button type="button">` for anything that acts. No `div` with `onclick`, no `role="button"` with a hand-rolled keydown handler.
- **`<dialog>` with `showModal()`** for the fit quiz and the mobile menu. Native focus trap, native Esc, native `inert` on the background. Hand-built modals are where keyboard traps come from.
- **Sticky-nav occlusion (WCAG 2.4.11).** Every section id gets `scroll-margin-top: calc(var(--nav-h) + 1.5rem)`, and so does `:focus`. Without it, an anchor jump or a Tab into a section puts the focused element behind the nav.
- **Focus ring, two-tone so it works on every surface:**
  ```css
  :where(a, button, summary, [tabindex]):focus-visible {
    outline: 2px solid var(--focus);
    outline-offset: 3px;
    box-shadow: 0 0 0 5px var(--focus-halo);
    border-radius: 2px;
  }
  :root { --focus: #00311F; --focus-halo: #FFF7E6; }
  .on-pine { --focus: #FFF7E6; --focus-halo: #00311F; }
  ```
  Pine on Beige is 13.5:1 and Beige on Pine is 13.5:1, both far above the 3:1 required by 1.4.11. **Gold must never be a focus ring:** on the beige it is 1.54:1 and effectively invisible.
- **`*:focus { outline: none }` is forbidden.** Only `:focus-visible` may restyle, and it must restyle, not remove.
- **Target size ≥ 24×24 CSS px (WCAG 2.5.8), 44×44 preferred.** The three at-risk controls on this page are the `+` / `−` disclosure markers in `#what-we-do` and `#faq`, the carousel dots in the hero, and the nav index numerals. Give each a padded hit area; the whole `<summary>` row should be the target, not the glyph.
- **No drag-only interaction (WCAG 2.5.7).** Source B has `Drag to explore ↔` on its 3D stage. If that survives, it needs `<button>` prev/next and arrow-key support as an equal alternative, and the drag becomes the enhancement.
- **No scroll hijacking.** Native scrolling only, which source B's own `DESIGN.md` already commits to. Do not add Lenis or Locomotive: both break Page Up/Down, Home/End, spacebar paging and `scroll-margin-top`, and they make `prefers-reduced-motion` much harder to honour. `scroll-behavior: smooth` inside a `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` query is enough.
- **Tab order equals DOM order.** No positive `tabindex` anywhere. The scroll-progress bar is `aria-hidden="true"` and not focusable.
- **Visible at 200% zoom and at 320 px width** with no horizontal scroll. Oversized display type is the risk: clamp it, `--step-6: clamp(2.75rem, 11vw, 9rem)`, and test the longest line (`that’s your advantage.`) at 320 px.

### 6.6 Screen readers, split text and decorative motion

**The split-text rule.** Every display headline ships two copies of its text: one plain for assistive technology, one split for animation and hidden from AT.

```html
<h2 class="display">
  <span class="sr-only">No case studies. That’s your advantage.</span>
  <span aria-hidden="true">
    <span class="dl"><span class="w">No</span> <span class="w">case</span> <span class="w">studies.</span></span>
    <span class="dl"><span class="w">That’s</span></span>
    <span class="dl"><span class="w">your</span> <span class="w">advantage.</span></span>
  </span>
</h2>
```

- `.sr-only` uses the clip-rect pattern, **not** `display: none` and not `visibility: hidden`, both of which remove the text from the accessibility tree.
- The visible copy is `aria-hidden="true"`, so VoiceOver reads one clean sentence instead of announcing each fragment.
- Both copies are real text in the DOM, so crawlers see the heading twice and the heading's text content is intact. That duplication is normal and acceptable.
- **Split by word or line, never by character.** Per-character spans multiply node count, break in-page find, and some screen readers still spell out letters despite `aria-hidden`.
- **The split is authored in the HTML**, not produced at runtime. Runtime splitting means a reflow after paint, a CLS risk and a flash of unsplit text.

**Everything else:**

- `<canvas aria-hidden="true">`. It is decorative. Its three-act story is already told in words by `#process`, so no text alternative is needed. If it ever carries unique information, that information is wrong to put in a canvas.
- Decorative glyphs (`✳ ↗ ↔ ◎ ▷ ↻ ▤ ×`) all `aria-hidden="true"`.
- The hero carousel mock: `aria-hidden="true"` as a single unit. It is a drawing of Instagram, not a control. If the build makes it interactive, it needs real prev/next buttons and an `aria-live="polite"` slide counter.
- The marquee: `aria-hidden="true"` in full. Its content is duplicated for the loop and would be read twice.
- Quiz result: `role="status"` on the result container so the outcome is announced without moving focus. The `Your match` ribbon on a level card is announced the same way.
- `<section aria-labelledby="...">` on all 14 landmarks, pointing at the `<h2>`'s id. An unnamed `<section>` is not a region.
- Section eyebrows keep their numerals as readable text (`(01) · The gap`). The numeral is meaningful context, unlike the glyphs.
- The `+` / `−` disclosure markers are CSS-generated (`summary::after`) so they never reach the accessibility tree. `<details>` already exposes expanded state.
- Live counter `[SPOTS] of [SPOTS] founding spots open` is static text, not a live region. Nothing updates it.

### 6.7 Colour contrast — measured, with the gold rule enforced

All ratios computed against the locked palette.

| Foreground | Background | Ratio | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--ink` `#00311F` | `--bg` `#FFF7E6` | **13.50:1** | Body, headings, everything. Pass AAA. |
| `--ink` | `--bg-2` `#F9E9DA` | **12.13:1** | Pass AAA. |
| `--ink` | `--bg-3` `#E3DAC9` | **10.38:1** | Pass AAA. |
| `--ink-2` `#183630` | `--bg` | **12.25:1** | Pass AAA. |
| `--ink-2` | `--bg-3` | **9.41:1** | Pass AAA. |
| `--bg` | `--ink` | **13.50:1** | Beige text on Pine. Pass AAA. |
| `--gold` `#E5C690` | `--ink` `#00311F` | **8.78:1** | **Only valid use of gold as text.** Pass AAA. |
| `--gold` | `--ink-2` `#183630` | **7.96:1** | Pass AAA. |
| `--ink` | `--gold` (gold as a filled surface) | **8.78:1** | Pine text on a gold button or chip. Pass AAA. **Best use of gold.** |
| `--gold` | `--bg` `#FFF7E6` | **1.54:1** | **FAIL everything,** including the 3:1 non-text minimum. |
| `--gold` | `--bg-2` | **1.41:1** | **FAIL.** |
| `--gold` | `--bg-3` | **1.20:1** | **FAIL.** |
| `--bg-3` border | `--bg` | **1.30:1** | Fails 3:1. Decorative card edges only, never a control boundary. |

**Hard rules that follow:**

1. **Gold on any light surface is decoration only.** Permitted: background washes, large fills behind no text, shapes in the WebGL canvas, a rule paired with a separately-labelled heading, the inside of an already-outlined form. Forbidden: text at any size, icon strokes that carry meaning, focus rings, link underlines, input borders, error or required indicators, the "Best place to start" badge border as its only signal.
2. **Gold as text requires a Pine or Forest surface.** 8.78:1 and 7.96:1. That is the pattern for the founding-offer figures (`[X]%`) and the `0` in the partnership section: put them on a Pine panel.
3. **Muted ink has a floor.** `--ink` at 55% alpha over `--bg` measures **3.50:1** and fails body text. Measured floor: **70% alpha** (≈5.4:1 on `--bg`, ≈5.0:1 on `--bg-3`). Define `--ink-muted: color-mix(in srgb, var(--ink) 72%, transparent)` and never go lighter. 62% is 4.27:1 and already fails.
4. **Control boundaries need 3:1.** `--ink` at 45% alpha or more. `color-mix(in srgb, var(--ink) 45%, transparent)` is the minimum for a button edge, an input border or a disclosure outline. `--bg-3` at 1.30:1 cannot do this job.
5. **Never colour alone.** The `Best place to start` badge, the `What we fix` flag and the `Your match` ribbon are all text labels. The three fit-check lists use `+` and `−` marks plus distinct headings, not red and green.
6. **Verify the computed values, not the tokens.** Any `opacity` on an ancestor, any gold-tinted overlay behind text, or any gradient under a headline changes the effective ratio. Spot-check with DevTools' contrast picker on the rendered page, especially where text sits over the WebGL canvas. If text must sit over the canvas, put it on a solid Pine or Beige plate. Never over live shader output.

### 7.3 Image and asset strategy with no build step

No build means no automatic resizing, no format conversion, no responsive-image generation. So the strategy is to need almost no images.

1. **Nothing raster above the fold.** The hero is oversized type, CSS gradients across `--bg` / `--bg-2` / `--bg-3`, and the shader canvas. This is what makes a 1-request, sub-1.5s LCP possible.
2. **The founder photo is the only real raster.** Hand-export two WebP sizes once and commit them. Explicit dimensions prevent CLS; `loading="lazy"` and `decoding="async"` keep it off the critical path.
   ```html
   <img src="/founder-800.webp"
        srcset="/founder-800.webp 800w, /founder-1600.webp 1600w"
        sizes="(max-width: 48rem) 88vw, 32rem"
        width="800" height="1000"
        alt="[FOUNDER NAME], founder of YGA"
        loading="lazy" decoding="async">
   ```
   Until the client supplies it, ship the `[Founder photo]` plate from source A as a CSS block with no `<img>` at all. A placeholder `<img>` with a broken `src` is a console error and a Lighthouse failure.
3. **Versioned filenames instead of a hashing pipeline.** `og-v1.jpg`, `founder-800-v1.webp`, `yga-signal-v1.woff2`. That is what makes the one-year `immutable` cache headers in §5.4 safe. To replace an asset, bump the number and update the reference. Never overwrite in place.
4. **Everything else is SVG or CSS.** Marks, arrows, glyphs, the device frames, the product covers. All inline, all `currentColor`, all `aria-hidden` unless they carry meaning.
5. **Export budget to hand the client:** OG card 1200×630 JPEG at quality 82, under 300 KB. Founder photo 800 and 1600 wide WebP at quality 78, under 90 KB each. `apple-touch-icon.png` 180×180, under 8 KB. Nothing else.
6. **No webfont icon set, no icon library, no sprite sheet.** Inline SVG only.

### 7.4 Where the weight actually goes

Rough allocation inside the 150 KB target: copy and markup ~42 KB, CSS ~38 KB, JS ~30 KB, base64 font ~12 KB, inline SVG ~10 KB, JSON-LD ~8 KB, `.sr-only` duplicates of the display headings ~2 KB. That leaves roughly 8 KB of headroom before the target and 40 KB before the hard cap. The two things that will eat it are character-level text splitting and a copied-in CDN library. Both are forbidden above.

Measure after every build:

```bash
wc -c index.html
brotli -c -q 11 index.html | wc -c
grep -c '<span' index.html          # split-span budget, cap 400
python3 -c "import re;print(len(re.findall(r'<[a-zA-Z]',open('index.html').read())))"   # rough node count
```
